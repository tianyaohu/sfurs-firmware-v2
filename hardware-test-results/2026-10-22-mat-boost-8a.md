## Offline: why high current does not move a stuck wheel (all of tonight's mat recordings)
- Hall-edge gaps while |Iq ref| >= 2 A (h4a-mat, h5, h6ff*, h6boost, vx8a recorder traces, 1 ms): of 271 gaps
  >= 40 ms, 109 end 150-169 ms after the previous edge (4-7 per 10 ms bin elsewhere in 100-150 ms); 107 of those
  109 edges go along the command. 150 ms is MCSDK's HallTimeout (MIN_APPLICATION_SPEED_RPM 0): until then
  HALL_CalcElAngle extrapolates the angle up to 60 deg electrical past the last edge at the last edge speed, then
  resets it to the sector centre. A wheel stuck just past an edge therefore gets its current up to 60 deg off
  (cos 60 = half the torque per measured amp); it moves when the angle resets.
